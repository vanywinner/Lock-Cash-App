Terms and conditions

  • What the service is. Lock Cash lets you save money that stays locked until a due date you choose. Your balance is recorded in our system. The money is received and paid out through M-Pesa.
  • Topping up. Send money by M-Pesa to the number shown in the app, then enter the M-Pesa confirmation code. Your balance increases only after we confirm the payment. If we cannot find the payment, the top-up is rejected and you will see the reason.
  • The lock. When you save, you choose a lock period. You cannot withdraw before the due date. Later top-ups can extend the lock but never shorten it.
  • Withdrawals. From the due date you may withdraw all or part of your balance. We pay to the M-Pesa number on your account. You will be asked for your password.
  • Early release. Only we can release savings before the due date, and only at our discretion (for example in an emergency).
  • Your account. Keep your password private. Tell us immediately if you think someone else has used your account. We may freeze an account we believe is being misused.
  • Wrong payments. If you send money to the wrong number, or a payment is made in error, contact support. We will try to help but cannot guarantee recovery of money sent elsewhere.
  • Changes. We may update these terms. Material changes will be shown in the app before they apply.

Privacy policy

  • We collect your name and phone number to create your account and to pay you.
  • We store a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We cannot read it.
  • We keep a record of every top-up, withdrawal and adjustment, including M-Pesa codes, so that balances can be checked and disputes resolved.
  • We use your information only to run your savings account, prevent fraud, support you, and meet legal obligations.
  • We do not sell your information. We share it only where the law requires, or with payment partners when needed to complete a payment.
  • You may ask to see, correct or delete your information by calling support. Some transaction records must be kept for the period required by law.

Data collection policy

  • What we collect: name, phone number, password hash, balance, due date, transaction history, and the time of your sign-ins.
  • What we do not collect: your M-Pesa PIN (never share it with anyone, including us), your contacts, your location, or your messages.
  • Cookies: one secure session cookie keeps you logged in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Who can see it: you, and our authorised administrators. Administrator actions on your account are recorded in an audit log.
  • Security: connections are encrypted, passwords are hashed, and repeated failed log-ins are blocked.
  • Retention: account and transaction records are kept while your account is open and for the period required by Kenyan law afterwards.
